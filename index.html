Academic Biography

Andrés A. Zúñiga is an Assistant Professor (Professor Adjunto) at the Instituto Superior de Engenharia de Lisboa (ISEL), Portugal, and a collaborating researcher at the Institute of Mechanical Engineering (IDMEC), Instituto Superior Técnico.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the Universidade de Lisboa (Instituto Superior Técnico) in May 2025, with the final classification Aprovado com Distinção e Louvor. His doctoral research addressed risk analysis in cyber-power systems using fuzzy logic and Choquet integral methods. He previously obtained an Electrical Engineering degree specialising in Power Systems from the Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral (ESPOL), Ecuador, in 2015; the degree was recognised by the Universidade de Lisboa as equivalent to a Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering in 2019.

His work spans reliability and maintainability engineering, risk and failure analysis, electrical power systems, and applications of computational intelligence. He has taught at ISEL, Instituto Superior Técnico, ESPOL and in professional postgraduate programmes associated with Fundación Canaria Universitaria de Las Palmas. His background also includes professional engineering practice and supervision of academic and professional master's projects.

Research

Research Statement

My research concerns the reliability, maintainability and risk assessment of complex engineering systems, particularly electrical power and cyber-physical systems. I have investigated failure modes and their criticality, including fuzzy-logic approaches to FMEA/FMECA, comparisons of quantitative criticality assessment methods, and methods for evaluating risks in power-system components.

A continuing focus is the treatment of uncertainty and the quality of evidence used in reliability and maintenance decisions. This also informs my current supervision activities in industrial automation, diagnostic performance, component reliability data and equipment degradation. My wider experience includes computational intelligence and applications to energy systems and industrial engineering.

Research Areas

Reliability, Availability, Maintainability and Safety (RAMS)

Reliability and maintainability assessment of electrical and industrial systems, including the quality of supporting data.

Risk and Failure Analysis

FMEA/FMECA, quantitative criticality assessment, fuzzy logic, risk comparison and multi-criteria decision support.

Condition Monitoring and Maintenance Decision Support

Diagnostic performance, transformer degradation and maintenance planning, including current supervised research.

Industrial Automation and Robotics

Reliability-related applications in industrial automation and robotic systems, alongside robotics teaching and supervision.
