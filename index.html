Academic Biography

Andrés A. Zúñiga is an Assistant Professor in Automation and Robotics at the Instituto Superior de Engenharia de Lisboa (ISEL), Portugal. He is a collaborating member of the Institute of Mechanical Engineering (IDMEC), within LAETA.

His research interests include reliability, availability, maintainability and safety (RAMS) of engineering systems, with applications in electrical systems, industrial automation and robotics.

Research

Research Statement

My research focuses on assessing and improving the reliability, availability, maintainability and safety of engineering systems. It brings together risk assessment, uncertainty representation and condition-based approaches, with particular attention to electrical assets and industrial automation.

A central research interest is the development of methods that support traceable engineering decisions when evidence is incomplete or uncertain, and their application to the condition assessment, maintenance and operation of complex technical systems.

Research Areas

Reliability, Availability, Maintainability and Safety (RAMS)

Reliability and maintainability assessment of engineering systems and components, including the quality and uncertainty of supporting evidence.

Risk and Failure Analysis

Analysis and prioritisation of failure modes, with emphasis on uncertainty-aware and multi-criteria approaches.

Condition Monitoring and Predictive Maintenance

Assessment of equipment condition, degradation processes, fault diagnosis and maintenance decision support.

Industrial Automation and Robotics

Reliability and maintenance topics related to industrial automation and robotic systems.
